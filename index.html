How an engagement runs

01

Discover

Sit with the business, find what they meant rather than what they said, and write it down for engineering.

02

Design

Test each requirement against platform capability and regulation with the vendor; say what fits and what doesn’t.

03

Integrate

Map interfaces, data flows and dependencies with the bank’s IT; run migration with integrity checks.

04

Deliver

Run SIT and UAT with the client’s testers, resolve defects with the vendor, see the release into production.

Selected engagements

BBVA, direct engagement, 2025–26

Platform capability assessment for a European bank

The bank needed to know what its capital markets OMS could deliver, and how it would connect to core banking. With the bank’s stakeholders and the vendor’s teams, I assessed capability, mapped the integration paths, and produced solution designs within what the platform and regulation allowed.

Bonds, equities, treasury bills and certificates of deposit moved onto a new trade lifecycle platform, with the bank’s client and position records. Result: zero data-integrity incidents in migration
