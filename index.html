Three problems I solve

“We bought the platform. We can’t tell if it does what we need.”

Capability-versus-requirement assessment against your actual product set and lifecycle. Fit-gap matrix, a ruling on each gap as configuration, customisation, workaround or not possible, and an effort-ranked remediation plan.

“It works in the vendor’s demo. It doesn’t fit our bank.”

Integration and trade lifecycle design into the bank’s existing estate: booking, settlement, custody, pricing, risk, client reporting and regulatory touchpoints. Interface inventory, data and message mapping, functional specifications.

“Delivery has stalled. UAT is failing and nobody can sign off.”

Delivery recovery on a platform already in flight. Requirement-to-test traceability, product and payoff configuration, SIT/UAT ownership, release readiness and sign-off criteria the business will accept.

Platform capability assessment for a European bank

BBVA, direct engagement, March 2025 to September 2026

The problem
The bank had a capital markets OMS platform and needed to know what it could actually deliver against its requirements, and how it would connect to core banking.
What was done
Assessed platform capability against business requirements. Mapped integration paths into core banking: data flows, interface points, dependencies. Produced solution designs bounded by platform capability and regulatory constraints. Sole interface between the bank’s business stakeholders and the vendor’s technical teams across multiple workstreams.
Result
Every gap between requirement and platform ruled as workaround or customisation before it reached build.

Four-year trading platform programme at a UAE private bank

Business analyst and product configuration, 2021 to 2025

The problem

What was done
Designed order placement, migration and trade lifecycle flows. Configured the full product range through to production sign-off. Ran the high-volume migration of client and position records. Primary client contact for SIT and UAT resolution, the enhancement backlog and sprint planning across four years.
Result
Migration completed with zero data-integrity incidents. Product range signed off into production.
